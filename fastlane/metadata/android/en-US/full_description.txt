Searly is a free, offline app for the ThermoMaven G1 wireless meat thermometer. It talks to the G1 directly over Bluetooth. You don't need an account or Wi-Fi, and nothing goes to the cloud or to analytics. Release builds don't even ask for internet access.

Pair once, prop your phone up in the kitchen and read the core temperature from across the room.

<b>What works</b>
<ul>
<li>Pair with a G1 without an account</li>
<li>Reconnect without pressing a button</li>
<li>Live probe temperature, five zones, ambient and battery levels</li>
<li>Start a Manual or Smart cook and change the target</li>
<li>Set the G1's volume</li>
</ul>

<b>Not yet</b>
<ul>
<li>Background monitoring and notifications</li>
<li>Smart mode ETA and resting states</li>
</ul>

Pairing makes Searly the G1's owner. To go back to the official app, pair from there again.

Tested with one G1 on firmware 25. Other ThermoMaven models (P1, P2, G2, G4) may work but are untested.
