Searly is a free, offline app for the ThermoMaven G1 wireless meat thermometer. It talks to the G1 directly over Bluetooth, so you don't need an account or Wi-Fi. Release builds have no internet permission, and nothing goes to the cloud or to analytics.

After pairing, you can prop your phone up in the kitchen and read the core temperature from across the room.

<b>What works</b>
<ul>
<li>Pairing without an account</li>
<li>Reconnecting by itself after the first pairing</li>
<li>Live probe temperature, five zones, ambient and battery levels</li>
<li>Starting a Manual or Smart cook and changing the target</li>
<li>Setting the G1's volume</li>
</ul>

<b>Not yet</b>
<ul>
<li>Background monitoring and notifications</li>
<li>Smart mode ETA and the resting and ready states</li>
</ul>

Pairing makes Searly the G1's owner. To go back to the official app, pair from there again.

Tested with one G1 on firmware 25. Other ThermoMaven models (P1, P2, G2, G4) may use the same protocol but are untested.
